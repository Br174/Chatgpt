MOTORLAB SERVICE INTEGRATION STATUS V1

STATUS
Canonical capability/connection-status registry for MotorLab auxiliary services.
Central authority: Br174/Chatgpt.

PURPOSE
Distinguish clearly between:
A. MotorLab policy/contract support;
B. provider/plugin availability in ChatGPT;
C. actual account/resource connection;
D. project-level instrumentation/adoption.
Never claim a service is operational merely because its CORE contract exists.

STATUS VALUES
- CORE_READY: MotorLab contract exists.
- AVAILABLE_NOT_CONNECTED: plugin/provider is available but connection not verified.
- CONNECTED_VERIFIED: account/tool connection verified and usable.
- RESOURCE_READY: required external resources/bindings verified.
- PROJECT_ADOPTED: a specific project safely adopted the integration.
- DEGRADED: connected but temporarily unavailable/quota-limited.
- DISABLED: intentionally not used.
