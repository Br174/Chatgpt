VISUAL / PROGRESS RULES
- every canonical status emoji is accompanied by its label;
- preferred form is `<emoji> <label> — <context>`;
- repeated fireworks/lightning are not user-facing level codes;
- 🎆 is an action/phase completion; 💯 is only true full verified closure;
- 🔍 is solution search, 🧩 is verified compatibility, 🐢 is slow-but-live, 💭 is high-level task analysis, ⏳ is legitimate external waiting, 🧪 is test/verification in progress, 🔄 is synchronization in progress;
- classic percentage remains `Progresso MotorLab: <N>%`;
- once a verified percentage exists in a substantial operation, meaningful operational statuses normally include the current N% even when unchanged;
- percentages remain evidence-based and monotonic and are never invented for visibility;
- 💭 never exposes or implies private chain-of-thought.
