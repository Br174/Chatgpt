USER_INTENT
- Preserve the existing MusicLab AI/search engine and expand it; do not replace it.
- Build a new Cover/Originali/Crediti brain with three pillars: COSA cercare, DOVE cercare, COME cercare.
- AI is the final editorial judge and may contradict sources; external services provide evidence, never hard vetoes.
- Prefer wide discovery and later classification over early strict filtering.
- Cover target: up to about 100 useful results; Originali: dynamic count, all plausible distinct versions by the original artist.
- Strong multilingual cover discovery, including adapted titles and related/derived works; do not rely on literal title translation.
- YouTube roles: direct basic title search on title tap; final playback resolver; last-resort discovery/evidence source when other sources are sparse.
- One progressive cloud archive: reuse and evolve current MusicLab D1; absorb the best Cover-Lab-AI schema concepts.
- Preserve uncertain candidates rather than delete them; user can listen, approve, reject, or request deeper verification.
- Add selectable coverage/strictness modes; discovery pool remains broad while display threshold changes.
- Use parallel specialized search missions, but not 100 simultaneous LLM calls; planner generates many strategies, workers execute bounded parallel waves.
- Zero-cost runtime constraint: no paid fallback, no credit-card-required dependency for core operation.
