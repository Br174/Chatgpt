============================================================
LOCKED PRODUCT RULES
============================================================

NO MEDIA PLAYBACK IN COVER LAB
- Cover Lab never downloads, streams, embeds, stores or plays audio/video.
- Platforms are sources of data, metadata, IDs, relationships and evidence only.
- Flow: platforms -> metadata/evidence -> Cover Lab analyzes/verifies -> archive -> Music Lab.
- Music Lab remains responsible for playback/consumption.

YOUTUBE REFERENCE RULE
- YouTube is a first-class discovery/evidence source.
- If Cover Lab discovers a cover directly on YouTube during its normal discovery work, it preserves/passes the already-found YouTube ID/reference to Music Lab.
- If a cover is discovered on Apple, MusicBrainz, Internet Archive, Web or another source, Cover Lab does NOT perform an extra YouTube lookup merely to resolve a playable video.
- Music Lab can perform its own normal YouTube/YouTube Music lookup in that case.
- If the same version is later naturally encountered on YouTube, that reference can then be associated.

NO GLOBAL COVER LIMIT
- There is no fixed total numeric maximum for cover/adaptation/version discovery or archive size.
- Per-turn limits exist only for latency/runtime/provider safety.
- Archivio Vivo continues discovery in future waves.
- Temporary saturation of one wave never means the global catalog is complete forever.
- Music Lab displays results progressively in pages of 20; 20 is a display/page limit, never a discovery limit.

AI ROLE - REGISTA AI
- AI is the research director, not merely a post-filter.
- AI self-interrogates before source search.
- It generates hypotheses and research questions about:
  * Italian and international covers;
  * translations and localized titles;
  * completely different adaptation titles;
  * countries/languages;
  * performers;
  * authors, adapters and translators;
  * years and metadata;
  * live/instrumental/remix/karaoke when relevant.
- AI knowledge generates candidate pistes; AI memory alone is never final verification.
- Real sources/evidence are used to confirm or refute hypotheses.
- Discoveries can generate new questions and searches.

CONFLICT INVESTIGATION
- Conflicting metadata is not silently overwritten.
- A conflict triggers an AI investigation and targeted source searches.
- Example: different years may represent composition, publication, first recording or an actual error.
- AI cannot close a conflict from internal memory alone; evidence is required.
- If evidence remains insufficient, the datum remains uncertain/in investigation.
