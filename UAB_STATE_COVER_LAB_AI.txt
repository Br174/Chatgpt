============================================================
LOCKED PRODUCT RULES
============================================================

MEDIA / MUSIC LAB
- Cover Lab never plays, downloads, streams, embeds or stores audio/video.
- All platforms are sources of data/metadata/evidence only.
- If Cover Lab naturally finds a version on YouTube, it may pass the already-found YouTube ID/reference to Music Lab.
- Versions discovered elsewhere do NOT trigger an extra YouTube lookup merely to resolve media.
- Music Lab remains responsible for playback and can do its own YouTube/YouTube Music search.

NO GLOBAL COVER LIMIT
- No fixed total cap on cover/adaptation/version discovery.
- Limits are only per search wave/runtime/provider for stability.
- Archivio Vivo continues in later waves and periodic rechecks.
- Music Lab displays 20 at a time; 20 is only a paging limit.

REGISTA AI
- AI self-interrogates first and is the research director.
- It generates hypotheses/questions on countries, languages, translations, alternate titles, performers, years, credits, publication nature and variants.
- AI memory alone is never final proof.
- External evidence confirms/refutes hypotheses.

CREDITS RULE
- Credits are fundamental data, not optional decoration.
- Composition and version credits must be preserved with source attribution when available.
- Important roles: performer, composer, lyricist, writer, translator, adapter, arranger.
- Missing/unverified roles remain missing or to verify; never fabricate them.

PUBLISHED RECORDING VS PERFORMANCE RULE
- A real single/album/EP/cataloged recording must be distinguished from a performance merely recorded and uploaded/archived.
- "live" is a musical/version type, not automatically a publication status.
- Publication evidence can make a live recording a published recording.
- Without publication evidence, a live recording is classified as performance_registrata.
- Ambiguous cases remain da_classificare until evidence improves.
